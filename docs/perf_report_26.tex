\title{Performance Report 26: Vulkan Engine \\
\large Ray Marching Meets Grass --- Two Grass Pipelines, the Fivefold Wind Bill, and the Per-Cascade Shadow March}
\author{}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Reports~17--25 audited the ray tracer, the water machinery, the raster core,
vegetation structure, the load path, the fullscreen tail, and the generic SDF
march --- but never the seam where ray marching meets grass. This engine grows
\emph{two} grasses from one anchor stream: legacy textured billboards plus
captured impostor quads (\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp},
2285 lines, 13 shaders) and procedural SDF grass clumps marched inside the
generic SDF renderer (\texttt{sdf/types/SdfScene.cpp:625--746} +
\texttt{shaders/includes/sdf/SdfGrass.glsl}, 346 lines). The mode switch is
exclusive (\texttt{MyApp.cpp:1503--1509}), yet GrassRaycast mode still draws
the impostor far field \emph{and} marches the near field, evaluates the wind
field in both, shadows both (up to 6 vegetation shadow draws plus one SDF
grass march per cascade), and reserves both: \textbf{$\sim$385\,MiB}
worst-case vegetation device memory (a 256\,MiB concatenation nobody
reallocs) plus the SDF slice, plus 47\,MiB of $256\times256\times60$
impostor arrays. Wind --- 3$\times$ Perlin plus ambient plus up to 4 tornadoes
per evaluation --- runs $\sim$24 vertex evaluations $\times$ 5 passes per
clump per frame on the billboard side and once per SDF step per candidate on
the march side, in different units, under different LOD rulers
(metres vs clump scales). The impostor selector burns a 20-iteration
best-view loop per vertex; the billboard fragment fetches 5 textures before
its first discard; density thinning exists only on the impostor side.

It finds \textbf{15 issues} (3 critical, 5 high, 4 medium, 3 low). Each
carries the standard technique and a copy-pasteable prompt preserving
validation-clean execution, Synchronization2, and runtime feature detection.
Scope score \textbf{5.1/10}; folding the grass seam into the combined estimate
at 10\% weight gives \textbf{5.9/10}.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Scope and Method}

One seam, never audited as a performance subject: where the SDF ray marcher
meets grass. Three parts: (1) the vegetation renderer --- billboard
near field, captured-impostor far field, merged GPU cull, bake, wind, and
its per-frame depth+color passes (\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp/.hpp},
\texttt{ImpostorCapture.*}, 13 shaders under
\texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/}); (2) the SDF grass path --- anchor
ingest, region-partitioned rebuild, the grass branch of the generic march,
the 4-entry wind-lean cache, blade-LOD and impostor hand-off
(\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:1418--1675}, \texttt{SdfScene.cpp:625--746},
\texttt{SdfGrass.glsl}, \texttt{SdfRenderer.frag} grass regions); (3) the
double shadow --- billboard EVSM plus impostor EVSM plus the SDF grass-only
EVSM march, each per cascade (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:665--759},
\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2712--2792}). Report~25 priced the generic march
(smoke container, tracer, resolve, normals); report~22 priced vegetation
structure (pools, submits, equirect). Neither priced the grass-on-grass
overlap. Method matches reports~22--25: full reads with file:line evidence,
\texttt{spirv-dis} word/instruction tallies synthesized with the
\texttt{Makefile} \texttt{glslc} flags (\texttt{bin/shaders/} was never built
in this checkout, so no shipped modules exist to measure; §\ref{sec:budgets}
documents the synthesis), byte tallies from creation code. The application
was not executed; dynamic magnitudes are analytic estimates from verified
structure at $1920\times1080$ (2.07\,M pixels), 3 frames in flight.

\subsection{Recommended Order}

Unify the far-field LOD ruler and kill the double shadow (C1, C3)
$\rightarrow$ cache wind per instance per frame instead of per vertex per
pass (C2) $\rightarrow$ hoist the best-view select, fetch-then-discard
order, and per-cascade march repetition (H4--H8) $\rightarrow$ gate passes on
counts, thin billboards like impostors, stage uploads device-local, decouple
consolidation from the frame (M9--M12) $\rightarrow$ hygiene (L13--L15).

$S_{\mathrm{cas}}$ the shadow cascades each paying both a vegetation shadow
draw and an SDF grass march. Reports~17--25 priced everything else; this
report prices the grass seam, the only term that evaluates the same wind
twice in two coordinate frames for the same plant.

\section{Critical Issues}

\subsection{C1: Two Grasses, Two LOD Rulers, Two Shadows for One Meadow}
\label{sec:c1}

\textbf{Severity: Critical.}

One anchor stream grows two grasses. Vegetation placement samples
brush-3 triangles at 0.01/unit$^2$ (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:2113});
the fence callback ingests the same anchors 1:1 into the SDF scene
(\texttt{:2039--2042}, replace on destroy at \texttt{:2076--2078}), where
each retained anchor becomes one Grass SDF instance partitioned into
512\,m regions with 24\,m cells (\texttt{SdfGrassConfig.hpp:24--28},
\texttt{SdfScene.cpp:699--744}). The mode switch looks exclusive
(\texttt{MyApp.cpp:1503--1509}: Vegetation xor GrassRaycast), but
GrassRaycast mode still draws the impostor far field \emph{and} marches the
near field every frame: \texttt{drawBillboards = vegetationEnabled} is
false, yet \texttt{drawImpostors} stays true whenever
\texttt{impostorDistance > 0} (\texttt{MyApp.cpp:2294--2296}). The two
far fields overlap under different rulers --- vegetation hands billboards to
impostors at \texttt{impostorDistance = 512\,m}
(\texttt{types/Settings.hpp:121},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert:184--187}), while the SDF march hands blades
to its own impostor at 48--80 \emph{clump scales}
(\texttt{SdfGrassConfig.hpp:52--53},
\texttt{SdfRenderer.frag:739--742}). Between 80 scales and 512\,m both
systems shade the same plants: once as marched blades, once as captured
quads. Both are shadowed too: up to 6 vegetation shadow draws per frame
(3 cascades $\times$ billboard+impostor,
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:700--755}) plus one SDF grass EVSM march per
cascade (\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2791}), i.e.\ up to 9 grass shadow draws
for one meadow.

\textit{Technique: one LOD ladder, one shadow.} Express both hand-offs in
the same unit (metres at the instance scale, evaluated on the CPU per chunk
per frame from already-uploaded chunk info) and make them complementary:
marched blades in $[0, N)$, captured impostors in $[N, M)$, nothing beyond
$M$. Draw the SDF grass shadow only for cascades whose texel footprint
resolves blades; let outer cascades take the impostor-only variant
unconditionally (the pipeline already exists,
\texttt{Makefile:220--224}) instead of selecting it per cascade at record
time (\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2757--2758}) while still rasterizing the full
proxy.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- C1: unified grass LOD ladder, single shadow}]
Goal: no plant shaded or shadowed twice. Steps: (1) convert the SDF
impostor band to metres per chunk on the CPU and align it with
\texttt{impostorDistance} so the march hand-off and the billboard hand-off
coincide; (2) skip the SDF grass shadow march on cascades fully covered by
the impostor band (outer cascades take the impostor-only pipeline without
rasterizing the full proxy); (3) skip the vegetation impostor pass for
chunks the march owns this frame. Files: \texttt{MyApp.cpp} (veg block),
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp},
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/sdf/SdfRenderer.cpp},
\texttt{sdf/types/SdfGrassConfig.hpp}. Validation: per-mode A/B identical,
draw/march counts per cascade down, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{C2: Wind Is Evaluated per Vertex per Pass --- Five Times per Clump per Frame, Then Again per March Step}
\label{sec:c2}

\textbf{Severity: Critical.}

The billboard vertex shader evaluates the full wind stack per vertex:
\texttt{applyWindSkew} (3$\times$ \texttt{perlin2},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert:101--155}) over \texttt{windSVF} (ambient
2$\times$ \texttt{perlinNoise3D} plus up to 4 Rankine tornadoes with
\texttt{exp}, \texttt{WindField.glsl:29--47,64--108}), times 24 corner verts
per instance (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1124--1140}), times 5 passes per
frame in Vegetation mode (depth + color + 3 shadow cascades) --- $\sim$120
wind evaluations per clump per frame, each with its own \texttt{cos/sin}
rotation (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert:192--194}) and Rodrigues tilt
(\texttt{:225--233}). The shadow expander duplicates the stack without a
\texttt{windEnabled} early-out (\texttt{VegetationRendererShadow.vert:79--112}).
Then the SDF march evaluates the \emph{same} field again per step per
candidate through \texttt{sdfGrassWindLean}
(\texttt{SdfRenderer.frag:794--814} $\rightarrow$
\texttt{SdfGrass.glsl:183--202}), softened only by a 4-entry round-robin
lean cache (\texttt{SdfRenderer.frag:586--599}). The bake header knows the
true multiple (\texttt{VegetationRendererBake.comp:4}: 24 verts
$\times$ 5--9 passes) and bakes only the height scale, not the wind. Worse,
the SDF \emph{shadow} march evaluates grass at rest pose (no wind inputs
bound, \texttt{SdfGrassShadow.frag:17--23}) while its AABB covers the lean,
so shadow texels pay conservative bounds for wind they never shade.

\textit{Technique: evaluate wind once per instance per frame, reuse
everywhere.} Bake the lean vector (xz + amplitude, 2 floats next to the
baked height) in the existing bake dispatch, which already runs inside
consolidation (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:437--469}); vertex stages read
it like \texttt{inBakedHeight}, advancing only the time phase per frame.
Keep the SDF lean cache as the correctness backstop for mid-frame time
changes, and document the rest-pose shadow conservatism as the bound it is.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- C2: baked per-instance wind lean}]
Goal: one wind evaluation per instance per consolidation, not 120 per frame.
Steps: (1) extend the bake SSBO with lean-xz + amplitude evaluated from the
same \texttt{windSVF} the vertex stages call; (2) replace the per-vertex
\texttt{applyWindSkew} chain in billboard, shadow, and impostor vertex
stages with the baked lean (time-phase advanced on the CPU);
(3) keep the SDF 4-entry cache and rest-pose shadow bound unchanged.
Files: \texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRendererBake.comp},
\texttt{shaders/includes/vegetation/WindField.glsl},
\texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.vert},
\texttt{VegetationRendererShadow.vert},
\texttt{shaders/includes/sdf/SdfGrass.glsl}. Validation: still-air A/B
identical, windy-frame A/B within tolerance, vertex ALU counters down,
validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{C3: The Near-Field Blade Loop Costs up to 24 Blades per Eval per Step per Candidate}
\label{sec:c3}

\textbf{Severity: Critical.}

\texttt{sdGrassClump} (\texttt{SdfGrass.glsl:179--344}) loops up to
\texttt{bladeCount = 24} blades (\texttt{SdfGrassConfig.hpp:33}), each blade
a 2-taper evaluation with bend (\texttt{SdfGrass.glsl:313--337}: 2 hashes,
root placement, \texttt{mix} toward wind, \texttt{sdGrassBlade} with 2
normalizes + lengths). The blade-count LOD (24 $\rightarrow$ 8 beyond
16 scales $\rightarrow$ 4 beyond 32, \texttt{:307--310}) and the skip logic
(\texttt{:297--305}) only help \emph{far} clumps; near clumps --- the ones
covering the most pixels --- always pay the full 24. That loop sits inside
the generic march's inner product: up to 256 steps
(\texttt{SdfRenderer.frag:524--540}) $\times$ up to 4 evaluated candidates
(\texttt{:751--780}) $\times$ up to 24 blades, with the SDF grass step law
deliberately refusing the \texttt{minStep} floor that protects every other
primitive (\texttt{:1092--1096}: a 0.5\,m floor tunnels 0.4\,m blades, so
grass steps at \texttt{dBest*safety} uncapped below). Report~25 C3 priced
steps $\times$ candidates $\times$ noise; grass replaces noise with blades
and removes the floor. A near-field grass pixel is the most expensive pixel
in the engine that report~25 never isolated.

\textit{Technique: bound the blade loop, floor the step with blade
awareness.} Cap evaluated blades per step by screen-space footprint (near
clumps keep 24 only inside a pixel-coverage tier, mid clumps 8, far clumps
the impostor cone at \texttt{SdfGrass.glsl:257--265}); replace the missing
step floor with a blade-aware floor derived from the instance's minimum
blade width (already in \texttt{params0}, \texttt{SdfScene.cpp:659--662})
so sphere tracing stays conservative without crawling at
\texttt{dBest*safety} through empty air between blades.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- C3: footprint-tiered blade count, blade-aware step floor}]
Goal: near-field pixel ALU down $2$--$4\times$ with no silhouette change.
Steps: (1) tier evaluated blade count by projected clump footprint (uniform
thresholds, defaults conservative); (2) add the blade-width-derived step
floor to the grass step law; (3) keep the 48/80 impostor hand-off and the
Lipschitz-exact rigid lean untouched. Files:
\texttt{shaders/includes/sdf/SdfGrass.glsl},
\texttt{shaders/renderer/sdf/SdfRenderer.frag}. Validation: close-up A/B
identical, step-count histogram down on grass views, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{H4: The 20-Iteration Best-View Loop Runs per Vertex in Two Pipelines}

\textbf{Severity: High.}

Impostor placement reconstructs the Fibonacci sphere per vertex and
brute-forces the best of 20 views by dot product
(\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.vert:74--99}, loop at \texttt{:88--97};
depth twin at \texttt{VegetationRendererImpostorDepth.vert:80--105}). That
is 20 iterations $\times$ 4 quad verts = 80 iterations per instance per pass,
paid in the color pass \emph{and} the depth pass \emph{and} every shadow
cascade that draws impostors (up to 3 more), plus a per-vertex
\texttt{cos/sin} for the un-rotation (\texttt{:79--86}) --- before a single
fragment is shaded. The view index depends only on instance rotation and
camera direction: constant across the quad's 4 verts, constant across
passes within the frame.

\textit{Technique: select once per instance, reuse per vertex.} Move the
best-view select into the cull/compact shader (or the vertex shader's first
invocation via subgroup broadcast) and pass the winning layer as a flat
varying; vertices keep only the placement math
(\texttt{:116--139}) and UV crop (\texttt{:149--157}).

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- H4: per-instance best-view select}]
Goal: 80 loop iterations per instance per pass $\rightarrow$ 20 per frame.
Steps: (1) compute the winning view/layer per instance per frame on the GPU
(cull shader) or once per quad in the vertex stage and broadcast; (2) keep
placement, crop, and segment culling bit-identical. Files:
\texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRendererImpostor.vert},
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostorDepth.vert}, the cull shader owning the
compact stream. Validation: impostor A/B identical, vertex ALU down,
validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{H5: Grass Is Shadowed up to Nine Times per Frame}

\textbf{Severity: High.}

Per frame the meadow pays: up to 3 billboard EVSM draws
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:700--711}, \texttt{indexCount = 36} via
\texttt{DrawIndexedIndirectCount} with \texttt{maxDraws = vegNumChunks}),
up to 3 impostor EVSM draws (\texttt{:746--755}, 6-index quads), and up to 3
SDF grass EVSM marches (\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2791},
\texttt{firstInstance = grassBase} over the grass container range). Each
vegetation cascade draw rebinds pipeline + 3 descriptor sets + push constant
(\texttt{:684--698,726--733}); each SDF march re-marches up to 256 steps
with up to 8 AABB tests per step (\texttt{SdfGrassShadow.frag:176--247}).
In GrassRaycast mode the billboard draws are degenerate (all verts culled
past \texttt{impostorDistance}) but still issued: the command count does not
fall, only the rasterized pixels do. Outer cascades march full blades unless
the prebuilt impostor-only pipeline happens to be selected
(\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2757--2758}, with fallback to the full march when
its module is missing at \texttt{:2772--2774}), even though shadows are
blurred and the config comment itself calls coarse LOD ``required for
performance'' (\texttt{SdfGrassConfig.hpp:40--46}).

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- H5: one grass shadow per cascade, impostor-grade outside}]
Goal: shadow grass cost tracks texel footprint, not cascade count $\times$
full march. Steps: (1) elide the billboard shadow draw in GrassRaycast mode
at the command level (not just vertex culling); (2) default outer cascades
to the impostor-only march pipeline and reserve the full-blade march for
cascade 0; (3) fail loud (not silent fallback) when the impostor-only module
is absent. Files: \texttt{MyApp.cpp} (shadow task),
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp}
(\texttt{drawShadowCascade}),
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/sdf/SdfRenderer.cpp}. Validation: shadow A/B per
cascade, issued shadow draws down, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{H6: Fragments Fetch First, Discard Later --- on Both Grass Pipelines}

\textbf{Severity: High.}

The billboard color fragment fetches albedo + opacity + normal plus
\texttt{albedoLod5} + \texttt{normalLod5} (5 samples) \emph{before} the
\texttt{weight < 0.3} discard (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.frag:60--62,94--95}),
then pays TBN normalize$\times$3, Blinn-Phong \texttt{pow(NdotH, 16)}, hard
shadows and a cloud-shadow raymarch when lit (\texttt{:114--156}). The depth
prepass twin fetches 3 samples unconditionally
(\texttt{VegetationRendererDepth.frag:36--38}). The impostor fragment pays
captured-depth reprojection per pixel (\texttt{invVP[layer]} matvec,
normalize, \texttt{viewProjection} re-transform,
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.frag:77--90}) before lighting, with no
depth prepass in the main view to reject occluded quads first
(\texttt{drawDepth} issues billboards only,
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1714--1723} vs shadow which draws both).
The SDF side repeats the pattern: surface normals cost a tetrahedral 4-tap
near (\texttt{SdfRenderer.frag:848--854}, report~25 H7) on hits the march
just found. Every pipeline shades occluded and discarded pixels at full
price.

\textit{Technique: discard on the cheap fetch, prepass the impostors.}
Test opacity/coverage from the single cheapest tap first and skip the LOD5
pair plus lighting on discard; add the missing impostor depth prepass to the
main view (the pipeline already exists,
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1417--1434}) so the color reprojection runs
only on visible quads.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- H6: cheap-first discard, impostor depth prepass}]
Goal: discarded pixels cost one fetch, occluded impostors cost depth only.
Steps: (1) reorder billboard/impostor fragments to discard from the opacity
tap before the LOD5 fetch pair and lighting; (2) issue the impostor depth
pipeline in \texttt{drawDepth} alongside billboards; (3) keep the EVSM
no-cutout contract (solid silhouettes, \texttt{VegetationRendererShadow.frag:5--7})
untouched. Files: \texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.frag},
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.frag},
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp}. Validation:
pixel-identical output, fetch counters down on discard-heavy views,
validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{H7: Passes Are Issued on Flags, Never on Counts}

\textbf{Severity: High.}

\texttt{drawDepth} binds pipeline + 3 sets + push constant + index/vertex
buffers and issues \texttt{DrawIndexedIndirectCount} even when every
instance is beyond \texttt{impostorDistance} or density-culled --- culling
is per-vertex degenerate output, not command elision
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1702--1725},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert:184--187}). \texttt{drawColor} repeats the
binds twice (billboard + impostor,
\texttt{:1741--1763}). Shadow cascades issue both streams per cascade with
\texttt{maxDraws = vegNumChunks} even when the cascade count buffer is zero
(\texttt{:706--711,749--754}; the GPU still reads the count). The SDF side
matches: proxy cubes rasterize over merged containers plus ray/AABB plus
1--2 depth taps plus dither even for grass regions off screen, and the
\texttt{MyApp} veg block transitions, clears, draws, and submits on the
dedicated vegetation queue whenever either flag is set
(\texttt{MyApp.cpp:2299--2407}) --- the empty grass mode (\texttt{None})
clears both targets and still submits (\texttt{:2365--2385}). The engine
already owns the elision pattern (report~22 C3, report~24 C1, report~25
M11); the grass seam is its last un-elided pass.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- H7: count-gated grass passes}]
Goal: no instances in range $\Rightarrow$ no draws, no clears, no submit.
Steps: (1) read back (or predict from chunk counts + frustum) per-pass
non-empty conditions on the CPU and skip depth/color/cascade draws,
target clears, and the vegetation submit when empty, routing the composite
wait to the cull timeline as the SDF-task elision does; (2) keep layouts
stable across skipped frames. Files: \texttt{MyApp.cpp},
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp}. Validation:
identical pixels on empty views, submit/draw counts down, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{H8: The 60-Render Capture Runs Synchronously, From a Lost Source}

\textbf{Severity: High.}

\texttt{ImpostorCapture::capture} renders 20 views per billboard type
inside one \texttt{runSingleTimeCommands} (a device-waiting synchronous
submit), each view paying 4 transitions + \texttt{BeginRendering} over
3 color + 1 depth targets + a 36-index draw + 3 transitions back
(\texttt{ImpostorCapture.cpp:241--337}); \texttt{captureAll} triples it to
60 renders plus ImGui set updates per type (\texttt{:346--355}), after
pre-transitioning 180 layers at init (\texttt{:67--79}). The vertex source
for that capture, \texttt{ImpostorCapture.vert}, does not exist on disk:
the live path compiles it from \texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert
-DVEG\_CAPTURE=1} (\texttt{ImpostorCapture.cpp:589},
\texttt{Makefile:230--231}), while the checked-in
\texttt{ImpostorCaptureLegacy.vert} (a pass-through to a nonexistent
\texttt{vegetation.geom}) has zero references anywhere. Capture forces
\texttt{impostorDistance = 0} and wind off (\texttt{:235--239}), so the
captured quads never match a windy frame exactly --- the mismatch C2's bake
would also fix.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- H8: async capture, delete the orphan}]
Goal: capture never stalls the frame; the source tree tells the truth.
Steps: (1) move capture onto the async path (own queue/fence, or split
across frames --- 60 renders need no single submit); (2) delete
\texttt{ImpostorCaptureLegacy.vert} and document the \texttt{-DVEG\_CAPTURE}
derivation next to the \texttt{Makefile} rule; (3) capture with the baked
lean so quads match the windy world. Files:
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/ImpostorCapture.cpp},
\texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/ImpostorCaptureLegacy.vert} (delete),
\texttt{Makefile}. Validation: identical impostor arrays, zero
frame-time capture cost, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{M9: Per-Frame O(Chunks) CPU Work and a Synchronous Consolidation Stall}

\textbf{Severity: Medium.}

Every frame pays for every chunk whether or not anything changed:
\texttt{recordReadBarriers} builds one \texttt{HOST\_WRITE
$\rightarrow$ VERTEX\_INPUT} barrier per chunk buffer (up to 4096 barriers
in one \texttt{DependencyInfo},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1219--1255});
\texttt{writeVegChunkInfo} memcpys \texttt{vegNumChunks $\times$ 48\,B} and
rebuilds the chunk-info map on dirty, called from both \texttt{prepareCull}
and \texttt{prepareCullCascades} (\texttt{:506,551--594,640});
\texttt{updateWindParamsUBO} rebuilds 80\,B and memcmps on every
\texttt{draw*} call (up to $5\times$/frame,
\texttt{:1599--1650,1710,1736,686}). Worse, \texttt{consolidateChunks} runs
the copy + bake inside a synchronous single-time command
(\texttt{:337--475}: N$\times$ copy plus bake dispatch at \texttt{:458}
plus 3 barriers), and both cull prepares block while it is dirty or its
fence is pending (\texttt{:480--484,608--611}). The chunk info buffer is
host-visible/coherent and persistently mapped (\texttt{:296--300}), so every
march-adjacent read also rides the host-visible heap path.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- M9: dirty-gated barriers, async consolidation}]
Goal: static meadows cost near-zero CPU; consolidation never blocks cull.
Steps: (1) record the read barriers once per buffer version, not once per
frame; (2) run consolidation on the async transfer path with a timeline
fan-in instead of a waited single-time submit; (3) collapse the wind UBO
updates to one dirty-gated upload per frame. Files:
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp}. Validation:
identical chunk contents, barrier/upload counts down on static frames,
validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{M10: Density Thinning Exists --- Only Where It Does Not Matter}

\textbf{Severity: Medium.}

\texttt{DistanceDensitySettings} defaults to full density inside 512\,m
falling to 10\% at 4096\,m
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.hpp:54--59}), packed with the hand-off guard
\texttt{nearDistance = max(fullDensity, impostorDistance)}
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1621--1638}) --- sound. But only the two
impostor vertex shaders evaluate \texttt{densityFactorForDistance} with
stochastic discard (\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.vert:61--70},
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostorDepth.vert:68--76}); the billboard vertex
and fragment stages never call it. Billboards --- the near-field geometry
covering the most pixels with 24 verts and 5 texture fetches each --- never
thin at any distance. The far field, already reduced to camera-facing quads
with a 20-view atlas, thins; the near field, where overdraw actually hurts,
does not.

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- M10: thin billboards, not just impostors}]
Goal: overdraw falls with distance on both representations. Steps:
(1) evaluate the same density factor + hash discard in the billboard vertex
stage (degenerate culled verts, mirroring the impostor path); (2) keep the
\texttt{nearDistance} hand-off guard so thinning never opens a seam at the
LOD boundary. Files:
\texttt{shaders/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.vert},
\texttt{shaders/includes/vegetation/VegetationCommon.glsl}. Validation:
near A/B identical, mid/far overdraw down, no LOD seam, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{M11: Two Shadow LODs in Incompatible Units}

\textbf{Severity: Medium.}

The SDF grass shadow march ignores camera distance and marches at a fixed
\texttt{shadowLodScale = 32} (\texttt{SdfGrassConfig.hpp:46},
\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2737--2749}), jumping to the zero-blade impostor
only at or above \texttt{impostorFull} (\texttt{SdfGrassShadow.frag:79--99}).
The vegetation shadow has no LOD at all: full 24-vert wind expansion per
cascade (\texttt{VegetationRendererShadow.vert:79--112}) with
\texttt{windEnabled = -1} merely signalling the fragment
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:690--694},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.frag:56}). One LOD is in clump scales, the other
in metres, neither follows cascade texel footprint --- yet footprint is what
determines whether blades survive the EVSM blur. The impostor-only shadow
pipeline that would let outer cascades skip blades exists
(\texttt{Makefile:220--224}) but is chosen per cascade behind a fallback
that silently takes the full march when the module is missing
(\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:2772--2774}).

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- M11: footprint-driven shadow LOD}]
Goal: each cascade marches what its texels resolve. Steps: (1) drive both
shadow LODs from cascade texel footprint (cascade 0 full, outer cascades
impostor-grade); (2) make the impostor-only module load failure loud;
(3) unify the units with C1's ladder. Files:
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/sdf/SdfRenderer.cpp},
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp},
\texttt{sdf/types/SdfGrassConfig.hpp}. Validation: shadow A/B per cascade,
outer-cascade shadow ALU down, validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{M12: Streaming Churn per Chunk --- Copies, Allocs, and Fence Callbacks}

\textbf{Severity: Medium.}

\texttt{processPendingChunks} drains 10 chunks per frame
(\texttt{MyApp.cpp:1128}) through per-chunk position/normal copies, a full
vertex AABB scan, scratch reservation, per-triangle hash/biome/random work,
a staging memcpy, a device \texttt{createBuffer}, and an async copy with a
fence callback per batch (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1854--2047}),
with the AABB unconditionally bloated by \texttt{billboardScale $\times$
2.1} (\texttt{:1871--1881}) --- bloating every proxy, cull volume, and SDF
region bound downstream. Each batch pins a staging pool entry at peak
concurrency that never shrinks (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.hpp:480--488}).
Shape edits (\texttt{setGrassClumpRadius} et al.,
\texttt{SdfRenderer.cpp:1545--1623}) set \texttt{grassDirty\_} and rebuild
the whole grass scene plus re-merge plus re-upload all 6 vectors for what is
often a slider drag (\texttt{:1480--1530} via
\texttt{SceneRenderer.cpp:1857--1860}).

\begin{tcolorbox}[promptbox={Prompt --- M12: quieter streaming, coarser grass dirty}]
Goal: chunk streaming and slider drags stop spiking the frame. Steps:
(1) size staging once from the batch total instead of per-chunk acquire;
(2) derive the AABB bloat from eventual lean + blade extent, not a fixed
$2.1\times$; (3) split grass shape dirt into cheap (tint/roughness: UBO
stream) vs structural (radius/blades: rebuild) so drags do not re-merge.
Files: \texttt{vulkan/renderer/vegetation/VegetationRenderer.cpp},
\texttt{vulkan/renderer/sdf/SdfRenderer.cpp}. Validation: identical
placement/bounds within tolerance, spike frames gone on drags,
validation-clean.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{L13: Near-Twin Shadow Shaders and a Shared-Vertex Opportunity}

\texttt{ShadowRenderer.frag} (22 lines) and
\texttt{VegetationRendererShadow.frag} (22 lines) both write
\texttt{(exp(2z), exp(4z))} EVSM moments, one inlining \texttt{exp}, the
other calling \texttt{evsmMoments()} --- one helper, two spellings.
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostorShadow.frag} (49 lines, 987 words) is a
stripped twin of \texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.frag} (135 lines,
14144 words) with identical inputs; the two impostor vertex shaders differ
only in varyings and sentinel convention
(\texttt{VARY\_TANGENTWS} vs \texttt{VARY\_FACE\_NORMAL}, \texttt{w = 2}
vs degenerate). \texttt{SdfRenderer.vert} is already shared by both SDF
pipelines (report~25 L13 resolved); the vegetation side never applied the
lesson. Dedupe behind one EVSM writer and one impostor vertex core with
\texttt{-D} variants, as C1/H8 already do for the SDF pair.

\subsection{L14: Small-Upload and Descriptor Hygiene}

\texttt{updateWindParamsUBO} (80\,B) and \texttt{updateWindFieldUBO}
(304\,B) memcmp on up to 6 call sites per frame to save one coherent memcpy
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:1544--1650}); the capture UBO strides views
at 256\,B for $\ge$5\,KiB total (\texttt{ImpostorCapture.cpp:713--721});
every vegetation/capture layout sets \texttt{UPDATE\_AFTER\_BIND}
(\texttt{VegetationRenderer.cpp:874--895,1315--1319},
\texttt{ImpostorCapture.cpp:557--577}), disabling driver optimizations for
sets refreshed only on texture-array events (\texttt{:796--811}).
\texttt{setImpostorData} calls \texttt{waitForFrameFences} --- a CPU stall
--- on a data path (\texttt{:1269}). Gate once per frame, pack the capture
UBO tightly, restrict \texttt{UPDATE\_AFTER\_BIND} to the actually-updated
set, and move the fence wait off the upload path.

\subsection{L15: Magic Blade Constants and Removed-Dither Lore}

\texttt{lip = 1 + amp\,(freq + 2/h)}, \texttt{amp = 0.30\,max(r, h/2)},
\texttt{freq = 2.5/scale}, \texttt{detail = max(h\,0.35, w\,6)},
\texttt{K = (maxR + amp)/lip} and the 24/8/4 blade tiers at 16/32 scales
(\texttt{SdfGrass.glsl:140--172,237,291--310}) are unexplained globals; the
removed $0.50\times$--$1.15\times$ Bayer dither is documented in three
places as the ring it left behind (\texttt{VegetationRenderer.vert:176--183},
\texttt{VegetationRenderer.frag:83--90},
\texttt{VegetationRendererImpostor.vert:51--53}). Collect the blade-budget
proof (max overstep $<$ min blade width at each tier, C3/M11) next to the
constants so the next tuning pass does not re-break sphere-tracing
conservatism, and fold the dither epitaphs into one comment.

\subsection{Overall: 5.1/10 Scope, 5.9/10 Combined}

\begin{equation}
0.35(5.0) + 0.30(4.5) + 0.20(5.5) + 0.15(6.0) = \mathbf{5.1}
\end{equation}

Report~25 scored the combined engine 6.0/10; that estimate priced geometry,
submits, pools, arrays, mixer, load, tail, and the generic march, but not
the grass seam. Measuring it:

\begin{equation}
0.90(6.0) + 0.10(5.1) = \mathbf{5.9}
\end{equation}

The engine's verdict is unchanged in kind, extended in scope one last time:
the frame-graph architecture (async queues, timeline DAG, cached
descriptors, specialized shader variants, elided debug tasks) is sound; the
grass seam --- the same wind evaluated twice for the same plant, the same
plant shaded twice under two rulers, the same shadow marched nine times ---
is where the money is. Landing C1--C3 moves this scope toward $\sim$8/10
with no redesign.
